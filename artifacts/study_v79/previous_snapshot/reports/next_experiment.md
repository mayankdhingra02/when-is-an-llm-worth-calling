All four improved LLM incumbents used the same owner level-7 transform/entropy pair and 2 MiB block. Read `kanzi_v78.md` and `results/v78_preset_diagnostic/summary.json`. More seeds on the same input cannot establish independent-family transfer.

1. **Prospective cheap-preset control on new workloads.** Audit public owner/recognized-corpus sources, licenses and workload relevance; choose inputs by a documented content/size rule before observing optimization outcomes. Preserve the generated V78 workload as exposed development data. Freeze a deterministic owner-preset/block-size strategy and a matched RF continuation, shared ten-evaluation prefix, seven search and three charged confirmations within twenty labels. Include an explicit rule for duplicates and invalid settings; do not retrospectively assign unseen outcomes. The V78-derived candidate is an exposed development choice, not an independent discovery. If meaningful paired advantage remains on new inputs, freeze the exact model/interface and matched-budget comparison before obtaining a new bounded inference allowance. Current V78 call/trial caps are consumed. Do not change prompts against held-out outcomes or silently lift limits.

3. **Independent families and deployable routing.** Audit additional usable software systems, group all variants/seeds together, and reserve untouched families before any controller fitting. Use only pre-decision acquired information; fit thresholds and utility on development families. Include never/always/random-at-matched-rate/uncertainty-only/benefit-aware policies and nondeployable hindsight diagnostics. Avoid learning a gate from five Kanzi seeds or pooling byte and millisecond outcomes without a prospectively justified utility. Existing routing implementations are insufficient evidence of new-model generalization.

4. **Reproduction and interpretation.** Rebuild and replay this newer native study in a clean environment; older cross-platform bundles do not cover V78. Separate actual multi-arm collection from modeled selected-branch costs. Freeze a useful effect threshold and resource tradeoff grounded in a use case; the existing 1% flag alone does not value storage against time. Complete a focused primary-source novelty audit once the supported claim is clear.

Decision criterion: continue a learned escalation claim only if advantages survive credible cheap controls and adequate independent-family evaluation. Otherwise develop the supported negative/mechanistic result. Neither direction guarantees journal acceptance.
