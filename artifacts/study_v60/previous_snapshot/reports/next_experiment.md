# Next research decision after V59

V59 completed 576 valid physical trials and 60 classical arms. No workload passed
its frozen hindsight-portfolio opportunity screen. The p10 result is method
sensitive: random and RF-LCB each find the recorded minimum in 4/5 cases, 3NN in
2/5, and their hindsight combination in 5/5. That combination uses the jointly
collected branches and is not a deployable 20-evaluation policy. The result does
not prove that no useful selection or LLM opportunity exists. See
[the complete result](workload_screen_v59.md).

**Single most important next action: freeze an independent-software-family design
with one deployable classical comparator and a fixed application utility contract,
before collecting more LLM responses.** A broader learned-router claim still lacks
enough independent development and evaluation families. Do not make another
within-family variant look like a new system or count seeds as independent groups.

Priorities, in order:

1. Audit candidate-family provenance and historical exposure, using source/schema
   metadata before objective inspection. Preserve current MongoDB/Redis/Storm
   reservations until that audit resolves their eligibility; no unexposed claim is
   certified here. Fix the workload/correctness contract, candidate space, outcome
   metric, group assignments and bounded admission rule before collecting timings.
2. Specify the actual deployable cheap continuation in advance. Compare it against
   random and other cheap controls at equal 20-outcome budgets. Keep any
   best-of-methods portfolio diagnostic separate, with its full collection cost.
   A future admission rule must distinguish limited search opportunity from lack of
   a reliable pre-decision method selector. Any change from V59 is a new protocol,
   not a reinterpretation or retuning of the completed results.
3. If an independent, utility-valid task has meaningful opportunity, prepare one
   frozen paired real-model experiment. Save identical prefixes; account for all
   outcomes, physical repetitions, retries, malformed responses, tokens, latency,
   and model/controller calibration cost. Obtain a concrete additional request
   allowance only after its exact prompts, model provenance, schedule, and caps are
   reviewable. Current V59 allowance is zero model requests.
4. Evaluate benefit routing only if heterogeneous real-model gains survive those
   comparisons. Fit features, preprocessing, thresholds and the controller using
   development families only; keep every related task/version/seed in one group.
   Otherwise report insufficient evidence. Do not promise a positive result or a
   journal quartile from another run.
