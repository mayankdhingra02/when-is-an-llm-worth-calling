# Research decision after V80

V80 completed all 105 real calls and 450 physical trials, with no invalid responses/fallbacks. The LLM never beat the cheap preset on compressed size: ten ties, five losses, and added decision time in all cases. Zero observed hindsight size headroom remains beyond that preset. This supports a narrow baseline-sensitive negative finding, not an opportunity to fit a useful gate to these 15 seeds.

1. **Highest priority: independent-family replication with a strong cheap baseline.** Audit an additional software system chosen by source availability, meaningful configuration effects, correctness checks, objective semantics and bounded local feasibility, before inspecting optimization outcomes. Freeze an owner/domain prior or other credible cheap comparator before the new model study. Do not choose tasks because exposed outcomes look favorable to an LLM. Group related versions/workloads/seeds together. Existing exposed systems are development data; they cannot be relabeled untouched test groups.

2. **Replicate the newer native experiment independently.** The delivered 3.2MB archive reconstructs saved outcomes and rejects corrupted metrics/budgets/counters, but it does not rerun native compression or the model. A clean environment/host rerun should use the exact corpus/runtime/model pins, retained failed-run denominators and resource limits. Same-host timing samples do not establish cross-machine latency precision. Raw corpus redistribution rights remain unresolved; use owner retrieval recipes rather than shipping payloads.

3. **Reassess the research claim before more routing work.** V72 and V80 offer two distinct native settings with negative results against cheap domain-aware controls, under their own objectives. Complete a focused primary-source novelty audit around the supported baseline-adjusted claim, not a generic claim that LLMs are never useful. Stronger/different models may behave differently; justify any new model/resource scope prospectively. Do not repeatedly adjust prompts or seeds on the exposed corpus until something wins.

4. **Return to a learned gate only with adequate independent evidence.** Need paired model advantages that survive credible cheap controls, sufficient independent development/test families, pre-decision features only, and frozen utility/rate thresholds. Include never/always/random/uncertainty-only/benefit-aware policies plus nondeployable hindsight diagnostics. One-family seeds cannot establish generalization, and a 1% descriptive size flag does not price inference time or production utility.

V80's approved allowance is consumed, with no new inference batch currently defined or pending. Read-only source/admission work and reproduction design can proceed under standing authorization. No paid inference, model download, cloud, publication, push or external contact is authorized by this plan. Negative findings remain valid outcomes; journal tier or acceptance is not guaranteed.
