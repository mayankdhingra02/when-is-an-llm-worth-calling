# V86: strong controls and the available routing opportunity

**Concrete result:** exact enumeration of 65,664 escalation choices across three completed software-family studies shows no observed quality improvement over the cheap comparator for any choice in RocksDB or Kanzi. H2 has only a 0.262% family-average hindsight gain over its fixed prior, no H2 seed meets its predeclared 10% margin, and its repeat-range lower scenario is zero. This is a scoped negative result for the evaluated 3B model, not a universal claim about LLMs or evidence that a learned router works.

This analysis used all 25 paired cases from the selected V72/V80/V85 batches: 175 historical genuine model calls and 715 fresh physical trials in those batches. No new calls, objective measurements or downloads were made. The 65,664 choices are mathematical combinations of those saved cases, not 65,664 experiments. Earlier trials and all research overhead remain separately recorded. All three families are exposed development data.

## Result interpretation

Kanzi illustrates comparator sensitivity: four of fifteen LLM cases beat RF on compressed size, giving an unrestricted hindsight mean gain of 0.255%. The cheap preset removes that opportunity completely. None of its 32,768 binary choices beats never using the LLM; 1,024 choices tie quality, including the no-call choice, while any actual call adds measured selector time. All three compressed-size repeats are identical. This is exact for the retained observations, not a theorem about future inputs.

RocksDB has zero observed-median routing opportunity against either comparator. Repeat extrema do allow small favorable scenarios, so the data do not establish true latency dominance. H2 has 1.230% hindsight mean gain against RF, but four of five final paired configurations are identical; favorable timing differences cannot be interpreted as better configuration selection in those pairs. Against the standalone H2 prior, only one of 32 choices has positive observed gain and the maximum is 0.262%. Timing noise, historical model-free prefixes and only three confirmations remain important limitations.

The empirical contribution worth developing is a **control-aware feasibility check before learning an escalation policy**: quantify what even a nondeployable best selector could gain, and how that changes under inexpensive domain controls. Hindsight analysis and strong baselines are established methods, so this is an empirical contribution candidate, not an invented novel algorithm. These results support neither a cross-system beneficial router nor a journal acceptance claim.

## Method and costs

For each saved case, g=1−LLM/control using confirmation medians. At each exactly-k call count, all binary decisions are enumerated with rational arithmetic on saved decimals. Independent sorted-gain and combinatorial expectation identities verify the maxima, minima and random expectations. Gains are averaged within each family only. Kanzi gives equal weight to its three inputs and their five seeds. Bytes, milliseconds and seconds are not pooled; 25 cases are three families, not 25 independent systems.

The H2 cheap prior consumes only three fresh trials and has no shared prefix. Its curve is **fixed-prior substitution**, not matched-prefix escalation. RF comparisons and RocksDB/Kanzi prior comparisons use the paired continuation designs. The figure and tables expose this differing control scope in machine-readable records. Do not present the two prior designs as identical treatment budgets.

Observed-repeat extremes replace each median with adverse/favorable observed endpoints. They are sensitivity scenarios, not confidence intervals or simulated new measurements. Material margins retain the original 5%/1%/10% conventions rather than searching thresholds. Hindsight quality ignores paid acquisition of both counterfactuals and is unattainable without outcomes. Added selector time omits prefix, native acquisitions, startup, router cost and serving; it is not full deployment cost or a dollar estimate.

Synthetic corruption fixtures and arithmetic tests are separate from measured data.

## What remains before a stronger paper claim

A realistic workload where credible cheap controls leave headroom, a stronger-model comparison, independent untouched groups, and clean-machine native replication are still missing. The best next resource investment is a single prospectively bounded realistic-workload development feasibility test, followed by model comparison only if it is informative. More seeds on these exposed families cannot establish generalization. `reports/source_followup_v86.md` records the current source leads and their limits.
