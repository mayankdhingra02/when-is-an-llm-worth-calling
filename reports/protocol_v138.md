Thirty cases, no selection by model gain. This follows the V136 projection diagnostic and is exploratory development, not a new independent cohort. No model calls or router fitting. Preserve every old outcome and failure.

Each original B10 prefix branches into two B20 classical arms. Fixed-prefix neighbor anchors on the best acquired B10 setting, then chooses its nearest unobserved configuration at every step. Adaptive-incumbent neighbor anchors on the best setting acquired in its own current branch. Both use Hamming distance over the same feature-only domains; anchor ties use observation order and nearest-row ties use the original frozen candidate permutation. Exactly one new label per step, ten steps. No new label influences the fixed anchor; adaptive labels only affect their own branch's subsequent choices. Retain incumbent. These are simple control heuristics, not claimed novel methods.
