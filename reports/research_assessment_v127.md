# Research assessment after the full-domain proposal experiment

We now have a concrete new negative result: giving this local adapter access to the whole admitted configuration domain did not produce useful average gains over strong cheap controls. This is a reproducible development finding for discussion, not established generalization or a claim that the research is ready for a Q2 journal. More calls cannot guarantee a publishable positive result.

V127 executed36real Qwen3-8B requests across all six existing families and five prefixes each, including six label-rotation diagnostics. It acquired900recorded outcomes across90paired continuations: model proposals, matched random feature proposals and full-domain batch3NN. Every branch reused the same ten-label prefix and ended at20evaluations. Historical full-domain sequential3NN and other controls retained their original costs. All cases and failures remain in the main analysis.

Two different validity defects must stay explicit. V42had already shown that V123/V124's later joint5%screen was impossible within their old shortlists; that omission is disclosed in the [attainability correction](attainability_v125.md). V127escaped that shortlist but introduced an output-capacity error: SACrequires at least520tokens just for binary/constant positions, beyond the512-token cap. Its five normal outputs and one rotation output could not complete. They fell back as declared; this is a design-caused failure, not evidence against model reasoning on SAC. The original all30-valid screen was therefore also unattainable. The vocabulary proof and a separate601-token synthetic formatting fixture are in the [capacity correction](capacity_correction_v127.md). These errors were found and disclosed after collection, not hidden by relabeling outcomes or enlarging a cap retrospectively.

The observed quality result survives an important narrower check. Each of the five families with valid normal outputs has a negative mean against sequential3NN. Giving all five SACcases the best admitted-domain outcomes from the already charged V126audit would still yield−4.718%against sequential,−0.656%against random projection and−0.366%against full batch. That is a hindsight upper reference with the other25actual outcomes fixed, not a repaired experiment. It shows that spending more calls solely to repair SAC's output budget cannot make these overall comparisons positive. It does not rule out a different intervention across the other families.

The five valid label-rotation pairs all changed their projected selections (overlap5,0,2,6,0of10); SAC's pair is unavailable. Thus this adapter responds to acquired target assignments in these probes. Responsiveness does not establish useful ranking, and the probe is not a controller. It uses no new outcomes and does not prove universal sensitivity or cross-hardware determinism.

The evidence is stronger than another prompt anecdote: actual paired branches, strict failure denominators, full-domain and matched proposal controls, exact source replay, explicit feasibility limits and independently reconstructed projection/ranking. The final suite passed1,005tests with14dependency warnings. The independent verifier's mistaken tie-break was repaired separately and regression-tested; no measured result changed. Corrected reports, metrics and figures reproduce byte-identically; a compact standard-library replay was executed. All prior evidence checkpoints are retained.

What is still missing is the central original contribution: credible pre-decision prediction of incremental LLM benefit on untouched software groups. This assay contains no new independent holdout, learned-router fit, cross-host inference replication or resolution of original measurement correctness/noise. The extensive adaptive development history must be disclosed; a frozen exploratory stage does not reset exposure. Candidate generation, projection and reliability gates are already prior ideas, so novelty cannot be claimed from this implementation alone.

**Single most important next research action:** freeze an independent replication of the negative mechanism result on newly reserved software families, with both input and output feasibility checked before calls and the same strong cheap controls. This should test whether the failure persists, not search for a favorable group or lower the quality margin. A source/novelty review must determine whether that narrower negative-result contribution is worth a paper before another long sequence of prompt tuning. Existing local permissions are sufficient for preparation; no paid endpoint is being requested. Q2 acceptance remains an external scholarly judgment, not an execution target we can honestly promise to reach.
