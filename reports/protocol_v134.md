# V134: explicitly retrospective attribution audit of the full-domain proposal series

This is an exploratory synthesis after outcomes were available, not a new preregistration, holdout, model run or router fit. Include every normal-condition paired case from the latest full-domain symbolic proposal series: V127 (30 intended cases, including SAC failures), V129 (10), V130 (5), V131 (10), V133 (5). Exclude label-rotation probes because their inputs deliberately mismatch acquired labels. Preserve them and their costs in original stages. Older shortlist/reasoning/model designs (V1–V126) are outside this method-defined scope, not erased; their positive exceptions and failures remain in earlier reports. Make no claim this is the complete repository or a representative population sample.

Question: how often does a final LLM-policy result improve the shared B10 incumbent, and how often does it beat continued cheap search? Both must be shown: improvement after a prefix does not establish value over the counterfactual, and gain against an initial anchor can entirely predate escalation.

Read only saved paired summaries and acquired prefixes/prompts. Do not open original objective tables, collect new outcomes, tune thresholds, select favorable versions or refit controllers. Preserve all intended cases, including fallback as the actually executed policy; distinguish valid model cases from failures. Direction comes from the saved task prompt. Group all seeds by the existing software family, never count seeds as independent systems. No statistical significance, population confidence interval or aggregate group-mean across heterogeneous task designs.

Compute for each case: prefix best, final LLM-policy value, sequential value, oriented relative gain from prefix, paired incremental gain vs sequential, validity/fallback, stage and exposure role. For native FFTW retain original selection-time prefix attribution separately from primary fresh-validation median paired gain; do not infer causal configuration benefit from identical-configuration timing differences. All other deterministic recorded/byte values use their original metrics. Exact Decimal/Fraction arithmetic on serialized values avoids arbitrary sign tolerances; compare back to original paired percentages to numerical precision.

Per-family descriptive table: intended cases; fallback count; policy improvement over prefix; improvement over sequential; >1% paired wins; mean paired gain in that family's metric. Also report valid-only descriptive counts alongside the complete denominator, not as its replacement. Family count/exposure and heterogeneous 512/1024-token contracts must be explicit. V132/V133 frozen decisions give only three prospectively evaluated new families; retrospective development groups do not become new holdouts. The SAC old 512-token representation failures remain evidence of protocol reliability limits, not evidence that valid proposals were tested successfully.

No new model/native/data download allowance: zero collection. Save input-hash manifest, exact case rows and source mappings, generated report/figure, independent arithmetic/direction/denominator replay, tests and a reproducibility receipt. This synthesis cannot certify Q2 readiness or establish novel routing. It can identify whether the current results support a narrower and reviewable negative claim.
