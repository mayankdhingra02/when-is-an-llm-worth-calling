## Interpretation and limits

H2 is one exposed development system on one generated in-memory workload and one machine. Five seeds cannot establish cross-system generalization; three confirmations and the range/median screen cannot prove equivalence. The strong predicate-index prior may leave limited headroom. Query time excludes setup/index construction; the separate deployment scenarios expose that accounting choice. All final configurations used the prior’s index mask 56; RF and LLM selected exactly the same configuration in four of five seeds. Differences in those four pairs are timing variation, not different final configurations. One RF confirmation set (seed 11) failed the coarse precision screen (range/median 20.75%); it remains included and flagged.

This does not establish a useful learned escalation controller or Q2 readiness. Hindsight min(RF,LLM) is retained only as a nondeployable diagnostic. Do not fit a router or select prompts/seeds using these outcomes. Stronger-model robustness, independent realistic workloads, clean-machine native replication and defensible novelty remain untested.
