# Research assessment after V113

There is a substantial executed, auditable research pilot. A reliable positive benefit-aware router and Q2 readiness are still not established. The latest work identifies a measurement limitation that must be resolved before stronger native-runtime claims.

Neither mode produced a valid>=5%win over both strong cheap controls. Thinking added cost and two final-format failures. ## What a paper could claim now

A narrow exploratory study can report that model escalation did not earn its cost on the tested recorded-data cohort, and that strong cheap controls, explicit inference failures, independent system grouping, and fresh confirmation materially affect how the result should be interpreted. The native timing negative control is a useful methodological finding. None of these observations establishes novel benefit-aware routing, general model inferiority, equivalence, population risk guarantees, or journal acceptance. Conditional escalation and budget-forcing are prior work, as recorded in the source audits.

The existing router implementations remain available, but there is little demonstrated useful escalation headroom and too few untouched independent groups to validate a predictor credibly. Hundreds of calls or additional seeds do not resolve that issue. Adaptive stages must remain labeled exploratory and must not be pooled into a retrospectively confirmatory result.
