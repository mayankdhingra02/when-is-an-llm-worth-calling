# Research evidence after V94 and V95

There is a concrete, reproducible negative-result paper candidate. There is
still no demonstrated benefit-aware routing advantage, and Q2 readiness or
acceptance cannot be guaranteed. The evidence supports a narrower claim than
the original proposed controller contribution.

## Actual new evidence

V94 prospectively added two native engines with correctness checks and five
seeds each. The protocol was frozen before timings. Qwen3's fixed non-thinking
selection had mean relative gains of −10.21% on SuperLU and −9.69% on HiGHS
against full-domain sequential 3NN; zero of ten pairs improved by at least5%.
Same-pool batch3NN also had better group means. All 500 configuration
acquisitions are retained: 485 valid and15 charged SuperLU worker crashes.
Independent replay checked all decisions/budgets and1,455 successful-worker
solution certificates. There were100 valid real model requests.

Across V91/V94, the fixed greedy non-thinking Qwen3 adaptation has worse mean
performance than sequential3NN in all eight groups (40 paired cases). Six are
historical/exposed recorded-data groups and two are fresh native groups; this
is not eight independent untouched tests. See `research_readiness_v94.md`.

V95 then tested owner-informed sampling and native decoding on existing
development groups, keeping V94 out of tuning. This changed sampling policy
and thinking mode together, with explicit control prefixes, so it is not a
pure thinking-only intervention or exact owner-recommended inference.

- Non-thinking:12 requests,12 valid answers. One case improved5.0046% against
  sequential3NN; batch3NN improved5.2326% on that same case. The model's result
  was also equal to the earlier greedy Qwen3 result. No valid model selection
  improved at least5% against both cheap controls.
- Thinking:13 charged requests. Twelve returned at the2,048-token limit
  without a final answer. The thirteenth, on sac, hit the120-second HTTP
  timeout. Its raw answer was not recovered. Five later thinking and six
  non-thinking conditions were never attempted, under the frozen stop rule.
- All36 intended conditions remain in the fallback-policy analysis; all360
  subsequent table acquisitions were charged. Do not describe the fallback
  gains as thinking-model achievements or unattempted cases as model failures.
- V95 original template preflight failed with zero generations. V95b corrected
  template control and stop metadata before scientific calls, with original
  records and shared limits preserved. Combined lifecycle1,460.178seconds,
  peak server RSS7,374,618,624bytes, clean shutdown, zero retries or spending.
  Returned responses contain24,816 generated tokens; the final server log
  records another2,048 for the timed-out call. Total observable generation is
  26,864 tokens, with no invented missing response.

All755 repository tests passed. Independent V95 replay reproduced all36
branches using360 saved cells and made zero new model calls or label accesses.
Figures are regenerated by scripts and visually checked. Original V94 and
earlier evidence is preserved through snapshot-aware seals.

## What can be claimed

Under these specific representations, local quantization, output limits and
evaluation budgets, escalation did not establish added value over strong cheap
controls. Valid answer format and a win against a single baseline are not
sufficient evidence of an LLM-specific optimization advantage. Longer calls
also need an explicit final-answer/reliability budget.

The development-trained benefit and uncertainty routers both selected
never-call for V94. They saved inference and missed no >=5% gains there, but
were identical to never and matched-rate random. H1/H2 learned discrimination
is therefore not demonstrated. This cannot be advertised as a successful new
benefit predictor.

## What remains untested

Independent hardware/clean-environment replication, a causal diagnosis of
SuperLU's panel-size32 crashes, more independent prospective groups, and
reasoning with an explicit final-answer reserve or the owner's much longer
recommended output capacity. Native timing noise, public-benchmark pretraining
exposure and mixed recorded/native workloads remain limitations. Larger paid
models, cloud runs and new external spending are not authorized.
