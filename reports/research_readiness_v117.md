# Research assessment after V116–V117

The defensible finding is **low observed practical routing headroom for the tested local-model adaptations**, with important limits. It is not a demonstrated successful benefit-aware router and not yet an evidence-based Q2-readiness claim.

V114/V115 remain the latest inference/optimization collection:36 real Qwen3-8B responses and480 recorded accesses. V117 reanalyzed those saved observations, with all12prefixes and6families retained. Against one B20 classical portfolio, no observed LLM continuation gained5%; the finite-trace hindsight branch selector gained only0.568% on average. That reference is non-deployable and does not estimate a population bound. Small gains remain real: nine wins, maximum3.34%.

A broader negative claim would be misleading. The single-portfolio average changes from−2.252% to+0.557% when OpenVPN is omitted, and batch3NN also changes sign under one omission. The sequential3NN mean stays negative under every one-family omission, from−8.248% to−3.384%. These are descriptive influence diagnostics, not confidence intervals or learned-router cross-validation. No family was dropped from the primary result.

V116–V118 pursued independent groups locally. It recovered the original BO4CO source and dataset DOI and ran feature/exposure audits, but admitted no new systems: source-to-target metric consistency and failure/validation linkage remain unresolved. Redis was already exposed in V60/V61. Passing feature coverage does not clear those prerequisites.

What now supports a reviewable study:

- Real local-model requests, all intended conditions, raw responses and usage, paired B20 continuations, strong classical controls and separate research/deployment accounting.
- Repeated answers nested within software families, explicit practical margins, preserved small wins, and a quantified non-deployable routing-headroom reference.
- Source/provenance audits and native timing diagnostics that limit claims instead of silently treating every benchmark row as comparable.
- Executed tests, reproducible figures and immutable historical evidence checkpoints.

What still prevents a strong generalization claim:

- No untouched independent-group confirmation of the final method/criterion.
- No useful learned benefit-aware router established on unseen systems.
- Local native timing remains noisy, with the second-host packet unexecuted.
- Limited models/interfaces and possible public-benchmark training contamination; source-derived adaptations are not exact SNAP2 replication.
- Novelty and venue fit still require technical review; repository size, experiment count and passing tests do not establish publication readiness.

V118 subsequently downloaded the original WordCount archive and verified its stated BSD-3-Clause license and all ten feature schemas. It contains no run-level validation/failure or executed-code manifest. The missing resource for the recorded-data direction is the original measurement contract or linked run records; downloading the archive again cannot resolve this gap. For native claims, the existing private second-host packet remains ready but needs a second quiet CPU host. Neither route may be substituted with favorable subsets or repeated exposed-cohort tuning.
