# Assessment after V141–V142: a replicated negative finding, not a validated router

The new experiment addresses model dependence with a matched comparison. It strengthens a concrete paper argument: improvement over an initial classical prefix is insufficient evidence that an LLM continuation was worth calling. That argument is supported here by two actual local model treatments, equal continuation budgets, strong cheap controls, explicit projection/failure accounting and reproducible source-row evidence. This is not a guarantee of journal acceptance or a certification of Q2 readiness.

Both Qwen3-8B Q4_K_M and SmolLM3-3B Q4_K_M received the same30 B10 cases from six previously exposed software families. We made60 genuine generation requests and acquired600 recorded continuation outcomes, producing60 B20 arms. All60 responses were valid; no generation retry or fallback. One failed SmolLM pre-start socket check was retained and repaired under a frozen pre-outcome amendment using the unused original allowance. It consumed no model request. Collection completed within the original1800-second bound, without new downloads or spending.

Relative to continued sequential3NN, mean gains were −5.5861% for Qwen and −5.5452% for SmolLM. Each had one win,14 ties and15 losses across30 cases. Qwen had a positive family mean only on BerkeleyDB (+0.0648%); SmolLM had negative means on all six. DUNE strongly influences the overall mean, so the report presents every family rather than relying on the pooled number.

Relative to adaptive-neighbor search, mean gains were −3.4577% and −3.4275%; each model had two wins,12 ties and16 losses. Both models had negative family means on five systems and tied on SAC. Neither model achieved >1% improvement over both sequential and adaptive controls in any case. This last conjunction is a demanding comparison against two separately deployable B20 methods, not a claim that a free deployable controller can choose their hindsight-best result. Qwen does have one >1% win against sequential alone; each model has one against adaptive alone. Preserve these exceptions.

Both models improved their prefixes: Qwen7/30 and SmolLM11/30. Their average prefix gains were about1.14%. Those positive numbers coexist with negative incremental value against continued classical search. This distinction directly bears on the original escalation question and is more informative than a favorable-prefix-only headline.

SmolLM's observed lifecycle was98.107s versus438.494s for Qwen, with peak sampled RSS3.389GB versus7.537GB. These are actual local collection observations, not portable latency/energy guarantees: model blocks ran in one order, tokenizer/templates differ, and cache/thermal effects were not randomized across repetitions. The average paired outcome difference nearly cancels across cases, but that is not an equivalence or non-inferiority result. Model size, architecture and training effects are confounded; no scaling-law claim is supported.

Projection is substantial:225/300 Qwen proposals and263/300 SmolLM proposals required a nonzero feature-space projection. Repeated proposals numbered52 and49; proposals matching previously observed configurations numbered1 and134. The domain-specific projection and grammar are part of the evaluated method. These results do not establish a universal failure of LLM optimization or an exact SNAP2 replication. Other prompts, reasoning settings, models and task definitions could behave differently; further variations on these exposed outcomes would remain exploratory.

The original learned benefit-aware routing hypotheses remain unestablished. This batch did not fit a new router, and none of its families is fresh held-out evidence. Sparse practical benefit limits the selection headroom; changing the classifier cannot manufacture helpful continuations. V140's native XZ case adds a separate prospective external negative result but cannot be pooled as an exchangeable seventh recorded-data family. Prior positive exceptions, invalid attempts, source qualifications and all collection costs remain preserved.

The highest-value next step is **one coherent prospective independent-family evaluation of the narrower incremental-value claim**, with admission chosen without candidate outcomes, fixed utility/correctness checks, the same cheap controls, practical margins and a finite stopping rule. Do not keep tuning prompts or sampling seeds on these six systems until a positive model result appears. Candidate source availability remains a real validity issue: previously used compressor families are exposed; Node.js has only a partial workload mapping. Independent-host/native correctness and realistic noisy-runtime evaluations address additional gaps. If a suitable independent cohort cannot be assembled, frame the manuscript as an exploratory controlled case study and seek methodological feedback on that scope rather than label it a generalizable routing contribution.
