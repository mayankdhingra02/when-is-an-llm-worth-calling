## Readiness and next decision

This is substantive reproducible research progress, including a small positive codec effect and a real prospective negative controller result. **It still does not establish Q2readiness.** Test count, protocol count and accumulated compute do not substitute for independent task coverage, meaningful benefit variation and a distinct contribution. H1/H2remain unsupported; zero-call policies cannot demonstrate that the benefit predictor selects better than uncertainty or random routing.

The single most important next action is to **extend the locked, correctness-checked comparison to additional independently admitted families before any further controller tuning**. Preserve WavPack/FFTW as exposed after this evaluation; do not reuse them as new holdouts. Admit tasks by workload/source validity, not by requiring a favorable LLM outcome. A narrower negative contribution about strong controls, tiny apparent gains and timing variability may be defensible, but its novelty against close original work and its external validity still require assessment.
