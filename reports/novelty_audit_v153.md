Do not claim a comprehensive literature gap, a first uncertainty/reliability-aware hybrid, or benchmark superiority over these methods. Their reported results are not rerun here and are not inserted into local measured tables. Generic LLM-inference-configuration papers and query-routing applications found by broad searches were not treated as direct experimental comparators merely because they contain matching keywords.

For a defensible empirical paper, prioritize clearly labeled BORA- and surrogate-rank-inspired checkpoint baselines, with acquired-label-only GP/surrogate computations and exact adaptation mapping. Full iterative BORA/LB-MCTS algorithms consume different requests and evaluation paths; our one-call saved continuations cannot silently stand in for their complete behavior. Any new policy fitted after inspecting current outcomes is exploratory and needs a new frozen test for confirmation. A negative empirical contribution remains plausible but unproven: when real model headroom fails to translate into useful cross-system selection, and how system grouping, output representations, noise and collection/deployment accounting affect that conclusion.
