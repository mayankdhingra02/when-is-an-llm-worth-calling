# Research assessment after V96

The study is more transparent after diagnosing a real threat to validity, but
there is still no defensible claim that it is ready for a Q2 journal. The
original hypothesis—predicting useful LLM escalation on unseen systems—has not
been established. Both fitted V94 routers selected never-call.

V96 adds40 actual native configuration acquisitions, with37 valid workers and
three crashes, plus a primary-source explanation of a memory-unsafe panel
statistics path. All three new crashes were at panel32;30/30 workers at smaller
tested panels completed. This is exploratory evidence on an already exposed
system, not an additional independent group. See `superlu_v96.md`.

This qualification matters for the earlier synthesis: the descriptive negative
SuperLU performance result was measured over a domain that includes this path.
Its raw observations remain true and preserved, but they do not provide a clean
comparison over a supported configuration domain. Correct solution certificates
cannot rule out unrelated memory corruption. The HiGHS result and older
recorded-data results use separate engines and are unaffected by this identified
source path; their existing scope and small-group limitations remain.

Highest-priority local action: a separately frozen restricted-domain follow-up
with fresh paired acquisitions and real model choices, labeled exploratory.
Independent-machine replication, more independent systems, and final-answer-
reserved reasoning remain untested. Do not discard original failures, tune on
the fresh-family results or claim that a diagnostic solves the benefit-prediction
problem. A negative-result paper might be possible after addressing these
limitations, but publication suitability requires a venue-specific assessment
and external scientific review.
