# V86: retrospective control-adjusted routing opportunity

This is a new exploratory analysis of already inspected V72 RocksDB, V80 Kanzi and V85 H2 outcomes. It is not prospective confirmation, a new native/model experiment, or a new learned controller. Inclusion rule: all completed native same-SmolLM3 finite-grammar studies with saved shared prefixes, fresh model-resident RF/domain controls, inclusive 20-evaluation RF/LLM budgets and three incumbent confirmations. These are the three most recent family-level studies meeting that scope; earlier stages remain visible in README and are not erased. No best-seed/workload selection. Family-specific adaptations and different prior budgets preclude a pooled effect claim.

Use all five RocksDB cases, fifteen Kanzi cases (three inputs in one family), and five H2 cases. Two comparators per family: RF and its study's cheap domain comparator. H2 prior is standalone 3-trial control, unlike 20-evaluation paired prior continuations elsewhere. Do not call the former a matched-prefix router action; label it fixed-prior substitution. Keep families separate. Kanzi summaries use equal weight per input and equal weight per seed (equivalent to equal 15-case weights here). Do not pool bytes with time or infer 25 independent systems.

For each case, g_i=1-L_i/C_i from saved confirmation medians (positive favors LLM). Enumerate all 2^n binary choices per family/comparator. At each exactly-k escalation count report uniform-random expected mean gain, hindsight best/worst mean gain, and mean additional selection seconds. All seven requests/case are charged in the estimated selected-branch request count. Enumeration uses rational arithmetic on saved decimal measurements; no rounding before comparisons. Cost is observed selector time only, excluding prefix, acquisitions, startup, controller and serving; full deployment estimates remain in original studies. Do not present these curves as total cost or an evaluated real-time router.

Also report the no-rate-constraint hindsight headroom mean(max(0,g_i)); never/always decisions; marginal candidate counts at the original descriptive margins (RocksDB5%, Kanzi1%, H210%). No adaptive threshold. Verify point oracle by independent descending-gain formula for every k, and random expectation k/n times the sum. Preserve all masks in CSV. Add repeat-range sensitivity: lower g_i=1-max(L repeats)/min(C repeats), upper g_i=1-min(L repeats)/max(C repeats). These are extreme observed-repeat scenarios, not confidence intervals, counterfactual reruns, or proof of statistical equivalence. Report unrestricted oracle bounds for those scenarios without claiming simultaneous realization.

No new LLM requests, objective acquisitions, downloads, external spending, thresholds trained on outcomes or held-out evaluation. Existing records have 175 real calls and 715 fresh physical evaluations in these selected batches; historical prefixes and all other research overhead are separate. Verify counts from records. Maximum analysis execution600seconds. Freeze code/input hashes before first aggregate execution. Include meaningful synthetic arithmetic/denominator tests, exact-regeneration checks and an isolated standard-library reproduction bundle. Preserve older evidence and update STATUS after completion.
