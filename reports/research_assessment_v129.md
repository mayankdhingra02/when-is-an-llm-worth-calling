# Research assessment after the two-family extension

The study now has another completed, reproducible negative result. On Storm and MongoDB, full-domain Qwen3-8B proposals did not beat full-domain sequential3NN in any of ten paired cases. This strengthens a limited mechanism finding; it does not establish the original benefit-prediction hypotheses or make a Q2-readiness claim defensible.

Every final normal response was valid: ten cases plus two label-rotation probes. Each paired branch shares its original ten-evaluation prefix and ends at20evaluations. Keep the matched random control visible when interpreting these behaviors.

The post-hoc [routing envelope](policy_envelope_v129.md) checks every one of the1,024binary escalation masks for these ten measured pairs. None has positive quality gain over never-escalating; eight tie, including never-escalate. The seven nonempty tied masks make extra model calls. Thus even a nondeployable hindsight router has zero observed quality gain on this cohort. Random mixtures cannot improve the expectation of these nonpositive gains. This finite-case statement uses no independence assumption and is not a population guarantee, calibrated risk bound or trained controller result. Fitting a router cannot demonstrate useful selection where these measured counterfactuals contain no beneficial case against the specified comparator.

The strongest defensible finding is narrower than the original goal: in these local-model, low-budget, recorded-label tasks, structural feasibility and label responsiveness did not imply incremental value over strong cheap controls, and the completed two-family cohort offers no observed benefit for a router to select. Novelty, independent generalization, native correctness/equal-utility and application-level deployment economics remain open. Journal quartiles do not supply an experiment-completion criterion or guarantee acceptance.

**Most important next action:** admit and prospectively collect a genuinely new, correctness-validated benchmark cohort before another router study. Start with a source/workload admission check, not favorable-outcome screening. FLAC is an unexecuted owner-documented lead, requiring pinned local source, an explicitly licensed fixed PCM workload and exact decoded-sample validation; it is not yet an admitted benchmark or a new result. Several independent families, locked methods and adequate development/test groups are still needed. Do not spend another prompt-tuning batch on these exposed families in pursuit of a positive mean. Existing local authorization suffices for admission work; no paid endpoint or new permission is currently missing.
