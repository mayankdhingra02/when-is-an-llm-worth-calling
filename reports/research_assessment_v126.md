# Research assessment after V124–V126

We have a reproducible development result about experimental validity and limited optimization headroom. We do not yet have evidence that a benefit-aware controller usefully generalizes to unseen software systems, or a basis to declare the research ready for a Q2 journal. Journal quartile is not an experimental acceptance criterion. Further calls cannot be promised to produce a positive scientific result.

Three substantive pieces of work completed:

| Work | Actual evidence | Supported conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| V124 local stochastic surrogate adaptation | 180 real Qwen3-8B calls; 143 valid, 37 malformed; 60 charged optimization outcomes | Repeated numeric predictions and EI were executed; EI and mean achieved identical final targets on these three prefixes, with HIPAcc falling back |
| V125 demonstrated answer-format probe | 18 real calls; both conditions 9/9 valid versus 4/9 in the corresponding original responses | Demonstrated markers improved format adherence on this small exposed sample; optimization quality remains untested for the revised format |
| V126 exhaustive admitted-domain audit | 4,932 new charged recorded accesses; 4,992 source values covered; six families, 30 prefixes | Only 4/30 cases can theoretically gain at least 5% against both batch and sequential3NN, even with perfect selection in the wider admitted domain |

The essential correction is that V42 already proved the original fixed shortlist could not meet the later V123/V124 joint5% screen in any of the 30 cases. I failed to carry that known bound into those experimental designs. The recent failure of that screen therefore cannot establish that a capable unrestricted LLM optimizer lacks value. The raw model behavior, failures, targets and costs are still real and preserved. The old criteria were not lowered or relabeled as passed. See [the explicit correction](attainability_v125.md).

V124's observed mean gain is −0.038% against same-pool batch3NN, −16.264% against full-domain sequential3NN, −0.336% against free first-ten and −0.174% against the single classical portfolio. The large sequential comparison includes a search-space restriction: for Dune seed11, even the best old-pool result has −45.630% gain against that full-domain control. Do not describe that difference as an isolated model or uncertainty-estimation effect. The same Qwen3-8B model/runtime was used in V123/V124; several interface/decoding choices changed, so their comparison is not a one-factor ablation.

The complete wider-domain audit now establishes that the restriction is not the only limitation. Mean theoretical gain over sequential3NN is 6.362% for BerkeleyDB and 20.762% for Dune/HSMGP, but only 1.618% HIPAcc, 0.455% LLVM, 0.121% OpenVPN and 1.067% SAC. Only BerkeleyDB (one prefix) and Dune/HSMGP (three prefixes) have joint5% opportunities. These are evaluator-only bounds, not successful LLM runs, predicted opportunities, probabilities of success within ten evaluations, or independent test observations. All five seeds remain grouped under their software family.

![Admitted-domain and original-shortlist bounds](../results/v126_domain_audit/headroom.png)

The source domains are feature-restricted and sometimes subsampled. The extrema are not unrestricted optima for the real software. Source performance measurements retain correctness/equal-utility/noise qualifications. This audit does not create new native measurements. The original V41 manifest's prospective-test labels describe its historical state: these groups are now exposed development data.

The useful research direction is an auditable separation of **available opportunity**, **model ability to exploit it**, and **pre-decision ability to predict benefit**. The first can be bounded retrospectively; the second needs real outcomes versus strong cheap controls; only then is routing meaningful. This decomposition and the finding are candidates for discussion, not established novelty. V42–V44 and prior work already examined pool opportunity; the contribution cannot be claimed merely by renaming that observation.

The single most important next experiment is a bounded, frozen **full-domain proposal intervention**, whose admissible feature-space projection is not confined to the old twenty-row shortlist. It must be compared against matched full-domain sequential and cheap proposal/selection controls before fitting another router. V43 already tested uniform20/diverse20 pools and V44 tested the 1.5B model on them; simply repeating those is not a new direction. Keep all six development families and the negative cases. Never select new test systems based on hindsight optimum or feed this coverage map into the optimizer. A subsequent confirmatory study needs genuinely untouched system groups, a fixed protocol, grouped analysis, credible measurement provenance and a distinct contribution against close prior work.

This session collected 198 real model requests, 60 optimization outcome accesses and 4,932 diagnostic coverage accesses. The two model lifecycle measurements total 1,165.888 seconds; coverage collection took 3.234 seconds. These are separate stages, excluding source audit, testing, analysis and agent time. Reused prefixes/control runs keep their original research cost. Hypothetical deployment of V124 requires 60 model requests and ten new objective evaluations per escalation; the retrospective 4,932-access audit is never a deployable B20 continuation. No paid/cloud inference, native-time or dollar-saving claim.

Independent replay validates the new requests, strict failure denominators, charged source cells, shared prefixes and direction-aware bounds. The final suite passed 980 tests with 14 dependency warnings. The V126 original verifier's presentation-order error was corrected in a separate file; original frozen code and failure are retained. Two deliberate copied-data corruptions were rejected. Reports, comparison JSONs and PNGs reproduce byte-identically.
