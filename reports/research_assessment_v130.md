**Concrete result:** a newly admitted native FLAC family now has a completed, prospectively frozen five-seed paired experiment. All303 configuration trials passed exact reconstruction checks across three real licensed speech clips; five real local Qwen3-8B responses were valid. Compared with sequential3NN from the same B10 prefixes and ending atB20, the model won0, tied2 and lost3, with mean relative byte gain **−0.1869%**. That is not evidence that the LLM was worth calling: every prefix already included the preset, the classical optimizer also improved it, and no model continuation beat sequential classical continuation. The model made additional progress beyond its own prefix in seeds23and71, so it was not simply inert; it still failed to beat the matched counterfactual. Seeds37and53already found their final incumbents in the prefix.

This addresses one gap in V129: it adds an actually executed new implementation with a pinned source, workload and losslessness contract. It does not create a multi-family held-out routing study. All clips/seeds remain one family, and the96-argument domain is small. FLAC is a different implementation from previously measured compressors but still a compression task; that application-domain relationship is disclosed. Same-implementation decoder validation, speech-only workload, one host/model, possible pretraining exposure and the long adaptive development history remain limitations. No new controller is fitted on five seeds of one system.

On these five observed pairs, a post-hoc hindsight choice cannot exceed never-escalate in byte quality: all five incremental gains are nonpositive. This is a finite observed-pair diagnostic, not a bound on unmeasured settings, unseen systems or other models. Existing earlier positive exceptions and the V129 result stay separate; do not pool differently designed stages or turn seeds into independent systems.

## Research decision

**Q2 readiness is not established.** More compute, a larger test count or this single new family cannot establish contribution or generalization. The original benefit-aware routing hypotheses remain unsupported by this stage. A credible negative result may still be useful, but its distinct contribution versus close prior work and robustness need assessment rather than repeated prompt searches for a favorable mean.

The single most important next action is to **freeze one confirmatory comparison across additional independently admitted software families, using the same strong controls and correctness contracts**. Admit based on source/workload validity before outcomes; do not require a family to produce an LLM win. Keep FLAC and every previous family marked exposed, reserve new families prospectively, and assess whether model benefit survives classical and matched random controls before another learned-router fit. If several independent families cannot be admitted within remaining resources, document that scope limit instead of treating repeated seeds as replacements.
