# Research claim boundaries after V76, before reading V77 comparisons

The target remains cost/reliability-aware escalation for software optimization.
A buffer repair is supporting infrastructure, not a substitute contribution.
These claims distinguish established evidence from what remains untested.

| Claim | Evidence | Scope / unresolved requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Syntax-valid LLM output can lack optimization benefit | V72:35legal SmolLM3 calls, five paired RocksDB seeds; no observed median gain against RF or simple prior | One development family/workload; cannot generalize to all models or systems |
| Early small-model routing can be degenerate | V6:three test families, benefit rule selected zero calls, copying/projection prevalent | Tiny model, constrained interface, few independent groups; not a general rejection of routing |
| Benchmark validity must be checked beyond accepted options | V75 application failure, V76 owner control/isolated-patch recovery | One Kanzi input/configuration, supporting validity result, not a routing result |
| Cheap optimizers improve Kanzi size within20calls | V77 collection in progress when this document was written | Unproven until complete and replayed; one artificial input regardless of seeds |
| An LLM improves Kanzi continuation sufficiently to justify costs | No paired Kanzi model experiment yet | Need identical prefixes, real model outputs, strong matched controls and explicit size/cost interpretation |
| A learned benefit controller generalizes | No convincing evidence established | Independently grouped tasks; pre-decision features; development-only thresholds; fixed held-out evaluation and precision plan |
| Ready for a Q2 journal | Not established | Supported coherent contribution, novelty comparison, breadth, uncertainty, clean reproduction and reviewer assessment |

Do not pool raw byte and millisecond outcomes. Do not pool historical models,
prompts, devices or defective runtimes into one treatment. Preserve denominators
for model/provider/application failures, charged confirmations and abandoned
studies. A negative finding can support a paper only with an explicit justified
scope and controls; more artifacts or a positive sign alone do not establish it.
