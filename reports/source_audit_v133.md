The existing SNAP2/EZR/MOOT audit remains in force; this native experiment is an adaptation. Prior report/source searches found no executed libjpeg-family optimization task. Library use to decode another task's images would not itself establish a native configuration exposure. The engine and all its related variants are one group; three workloads are not three independent systems.

- [libjpeg-turbo 3.1.2 owner tag](https://github.com/libjpeg-turbo/libjpeg-turbo/tree/3.1.2): downloaded and inspected original `doc/usage.txt`, `doc/wizard.txt`, `src/cjpeg.c`, `src/jcmaster.c` and CMake targets before collection. Lossless prediction, point transform, explicit RGB scan scripts and automatic Huffman optimization establish the actual domain. The predictor-sweep comparator follows from the seven documented predictors. The fixed predictor-7 reference is not claimed optimal. [Versioned manual](https://github.com/libjpeg-turbo/libjpeg-turbo/blob/3.1.2/doc/wizard.txt).
