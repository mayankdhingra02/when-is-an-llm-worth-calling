# Research assessment after V139–V140

There is another completed, prospectively specified native result. It strengthens a narrow negative finding about incremental LLM value, but does not establish a successful benefit-aware controller or justify calling the research Q2-ready. Journal quartile is not an experimental stopping rule, and acceptance cannot be certified from these runs.

V140 adds one newly studied software family, XZ5.8.4, with five fixed seeds and six equal-budget B20 arms branching from each saved B10 prefix. We actually executed 353 configurations, 1,059 compressions and 1,059 decompressions, including three separately charged reference checks. Every decoded file matched the complete original bytes. Five genuine local Qwen3-8B calls completed without retries or fallback. These are native measurements, not table lookups or synthetic responses.

The model improved none of the five prefixes; continued sequential3NN improved three. The model's mean relative gain against sequential was −0.0815%, with zero wins, two ties and three losses. Adaptive-neighbor and predeclared context-sweep controls attained the same final values as sequential. The differences are below the prospectively fixed 1% practical margin. The model's roughly 0.108% advantage over fixed references was already present in the B10 prefixes: attributing that advantage to escalation would be incorrect.

Frozen V132 benefit and uncertainty policies both selected zero calls. They matched never-escalate, as did the realized-rate random policy and hindsight diagnostic. This is compatible with avoiding unnecessary inference in this case, but provides no separation between useful selection and a trivial zero-call policy. Five seeds are repeated observations of one system, not five independent transfer tests. Workloads were previously used with another codec, and XZ's deterministic compressed-size target differs from noisy latency targets.

The strongest combined observation remains methodological: apparent improvement over an initial prefix needs to survive continued classical search and inexpensive local-search controls at an equal acquisition budget. V138 adaptive neighbor beat historical valid one-shot model outcomes in 15 of 30 system/seed cases, tied 14 and lost one, while still losing family means against sequential on four of six systems. V140 prospectively carries these controls into a new native family and finds no incremental model benefit. These studies are not an exchangeable pooled confirmatory sample. All earlier positive exceptions, model failures, repaired treatments and original collection costs remain part of the evidence.

V139 partially resolves the Node.js artifact map: the paper-linked deposit maps 0.csv to assert/deepequal-buffer.js, and archive/content hashes match. The eight parameterized benchmark cases and the 1,932-versus-1,939 preprocessing discrepancy remain unresolved. No recorded Node.js task was admitted, and no target values were inspected in this source audit. XZ was a newly frozen native task, not a relabeling of the unresolved old XZ table.

The next necessary advance is a coherent prospective evaluation across independent software families, with common admission criteria and group-level uncertainty. Do not select families because their observed LLM gains are positive. Before new outcomes, freeze the target claim, practical cost/effect thresholds, family-level analysis and stopping rule. Retain the current cheap controls and full failure denominator. Second-model and independent-host/decoder replication remain separate scope gaps; neither more seeds in XZ nor more prompt variants on exposed data repairs them.

The research is suitable for a substantive discussion with a supervisor as an exploratory empirical study with explicit reproducibility and cost accounting. It is not yet evidence that a learned escalation policy generalizes. If a coherent independent cohort cannot be assembled, narrow the paper claim to the controlled case studies and methodological limitations rather than labeling the existing controller validated. No memory, paid-service or external approval blocker is currently pending. The bounded V140 batch is complete and its limits are closed; no process continues outside the active session.
