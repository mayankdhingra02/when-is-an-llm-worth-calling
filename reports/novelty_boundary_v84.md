# Focused prior-work check during H2 collection

Checked 2026-09-26 against primary papers and author-linked repositories. This is a focused update, not a systematic review or proof of novelty. No study code, model, data or dependency was downloaded or executed from these repositories. This review did not change frozen V84 methods.

- **Chih-Yu Chang, Milad Azvar, Chinedum Okwudire, Raed Al Kontar. LLINBO: Trustworthy LLM-in-the-Loop Bayesian Optimization.** [arXiv2505.14756v2,9 October2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.14756v2). Methods2.3–2.5 inspected. Transient selection shifts weight from LLM suggestions toward GP proposals; Justify rejects an already-produced LLM suggestion using an acquisition-value condition; Constrained modifies the surrogate using that suggestion. [Author-linked repository](https://github.com/UMDataScienceLab/LLM-in-the-Loop-BO) resolves; no commit/license audit or execution here. **Boundary:** hybrid choice and reliability-aware rejection are established related ideas. Our proposed pre-call incremental-benefit gate differs from rejecting a proposal after obtaining it, but that distinction alone does not prove a publishable contribution. Their mathematical guarantees do not transfer to our finite RF/domain experiment.

- **Adam Rychert, Gasper Spagnolo, Evgenii Posashkov. Reproducibility Study of Large Language Model Bayesian Optimization.** [arXiv2511.18891v1,24 November2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.18891v1). Methods and model-capacity discussion inspected. They report replication with Llama3.1 70B and problems with smaller8B/27B backbones under their own prompting/parsing setup. [Author-linked repository](https://github.com/spagnoloG/llambo-reproducibility) resolves and describes the reproduced pipeline; commit/license not audited here. **Boundary:** our3B negative findings cannot imply that capable LLM optimizers generally fail. Their capacity observations concern a different pipeline and do not prove that any particular larger model will help our task.

- **Xinyue Yang, Chen Zheng, Yaoyang Hou, Renhao Zhang, Yinyan Zhang, Heng Zhang. PLRTune: Importance Pre-Sampling and LLM-Guided Reinforcement Learning for Automatic Database Tuning.** [arXiv2606.14312v1,12 June2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14312v1). Metadata/abstract verified only. It describes staged domain-informed DB tuning and refinement after initialization. Full method, artifact, licensing and claims remain unaudited; treat as a close lead, not an executed comparator.

**Decision:** preserve the narrow question about incremental benefit over a credible cheap continuation. H2 feasibility/optimization is infrastructure toward that question, not new evidence for an LLM gate. A paper claiming general LLM failure or first hybrid optimizer would overreach. The remaining critical evidence is paired real-model benefit/cost on adequate independent groups, plus a defensible contribution relative to these methods. Existing small-model negative results may motivate a bounded replication claim, but journal readiness remains unproven.
